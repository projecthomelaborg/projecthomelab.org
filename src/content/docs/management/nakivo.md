## Introduction

Nakivo is a cheaper alternative to Veeam, offering VM based backups of VMware, Hyper-V and Amazon EC2.

## Pros and Cons

One benefits of Nakivo, is that it offers repository level deduplication and compression, as well as the ability to install directly on to a NAS box to have a Backup-in-a-box solution.

On the downside, it does not yet support physical servers

## Costs

Nakivo comes in 5 editions:

- Basic
- Pro Essentials
- Enterprise Essentials
- Pro
- Enterprise

Features and pricing is available [here](https://www.nakivo.com/how-to-buy/vmware-hyper-v-pricing/)
