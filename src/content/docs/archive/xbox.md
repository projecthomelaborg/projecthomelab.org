## Introduction

<figure class="wiki-figure" style="max-width:250px"><img src="/images/4/49/Xboxone.png" alt="XBox One" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>XBox One</figcaption></figure>

With the introduction of HTML5-based clients for the [ESXi](/hypervisors/vmware-vsphere/) host (and an equivalent vCenter client in the Fling stage) there's no longer the need to restrict your HomeLab management platform to a traditional computer with a flash-compatible browser installed. Ladies and Gentleman, I propose the Xbox One.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- You can manage your Virtual Environment on the big screen from the comfort of your armchair.
- You have an excuse to get an Xbox if you don't already have one. Or a second one if your current Xbox is tied up by the kids.

### Cons

- Some functionality is currently limited- uploading files to datastores for example requires Chrome which isn't available on the XBox One yet.
- The Management interface can be rendered inaccessible by the presence of children.
- Sometimes you may get distracted from your HomeLab because you need to [escape from the Pillar of Autumn](http://halo.wikia.com/wiki/UNSC_Pillar_of_Autumn) or [deal with a zombie outbreak](http://minecraft.gamepedia.com/Monster_Spawner)

## Costs

The Xbox one is available from all major retailers, please check for current local pricing.

## Use Cases

The platform is ideal for monitoring ongoing tasks or performing minor management tasks without having to use a traditional computing device.
