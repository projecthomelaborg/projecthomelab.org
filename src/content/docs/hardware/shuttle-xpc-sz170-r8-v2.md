After Many research about new home lab HW for a VMWare Cluster a finished to buy this Hardware components.  
I am running 3 of this Cubes with VMWare ESXi 6.7.

**Pros:**

- Low power consume (CPU 65W - 3 of this cubes, 1x 24Port-PoE 16Port-10Gbit Switch and a 8Bay NAS = 200Watt)
- 64Gb Memory
- 2x 1Gbit Intel NIC onboard - Fully supported in VMWare ESXi 6.7
- SATA and M2 Storage support.
- Extremely quite
- Enough PCI-Express Slots for NICs or other

**Cons:**

- No IPMI
- No Intel® Active Management Technology - like IMPI
