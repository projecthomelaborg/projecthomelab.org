You don't always need your own lab. Here are some tried and tested free places to learn. Offers change often, so if something here is gone or has started charging, please [update this page](/contribute/).

## Free online labs

- [VMware Hands-on Labs](https://labs.hol.vmware.com/): real VMware environments in your browser.
- [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/): free courses, many with sandboxes.
- [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/): free AWS courses, with labs on paid plans.
- [Google Cloud Skills Boost](https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/): some labs are free, and others use credits.
- [Killercoda](https://killercoda.com/): free interactive scenarios for Kubernetes and Linux.
- [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/) and [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com/): cybersecurity labs with free tiers.
- [Microsoft Evaluation Center](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/evalcenter/): free evaluation software to use in your own lab.

## Courses and books

- [Linux Journey](https://linuxjourney.com/): a friendly introduction to Linux.
- [Professor Messer](https://www.professormesser.com/): free video courses for CompTIA certifications.
- [Cybrary](https://www.cybrary.it/): security courses, with free and paid plans.
- [Red Hat Developer](https://developers.redhat.com/): free developer subscription and books. It includes RHEL for personal use.
- [Packt free learning](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/free-ebook): a free eBook offer. Check for the current terms.
- Your public library: many offer free access to LinkedIn Learning, or O'Reilly, with your library card.

## YouTube channels

- [ServeTheHome](https://www.youtube.com/@ServeTheHomeVideo): hardware reviews, especially servers and mini PCs.
- [Jeff Geerling](https://www.youtube.com/@JeffGeerling): Raspberry Pi, Ansible and hardware.
- [Techno Tim](https://www.youtube.com/@TechnoTim): homelab tutorials, including Kubernetes.
- [Lawrence Systems](https://www.youtube.com/@LAWRENCESYSTEMS): networking, firewalls, ZFS and XCP-ng.
- [Christian Lempa](https://www.youtube.com/@christianlempa): Docker, Kubernetes and security.
- [Craft Computing](https://www.youtube.com/@CraftComputing): affordable used hardware and servers.
- [Eli the Computer Guy](https://www.youtube.com/@elithecomputerguy): a long-running IT education channel.

## Podcasts

- [Self-Hosted](https://selfhosted.show/): self-hosting and home infrastructure.
- [Security Now](https://www.grc.com/securitynow.htm): a long-running weekly security show.
- [Packet Pushers](https://packetpushers.net/): networking.

## Communities

- [Project Homelab forums](https://forums.projecthomelab.org)
- [r/homelab](https://www.reddit.com/r/homelab/) and [r/selfhosted](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/)
- [ServeTheHome forums](https://forums.servethehome.com/): very useful for used hardware.
- [awesome-selfhosted](https://github.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted): a huge list of software to try.
- Your local user group. Many of them, such as [VMUG](https://www.vmug.com/), hold regular meetings. This project began at one.

## Training trials

Paid platforms such as [Pluralsight](https://www.pluralsight.com/) and [KodeKloud](https://kodekloud.com/) offer trials or free tiers from time to time, and they're worth a look before an exam.
