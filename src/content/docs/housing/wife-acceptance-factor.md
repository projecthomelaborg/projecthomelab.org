Helpfully Wikipedia has already defined the WAF for us here: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wife_acceptance_factor>

*“design elements that increase the likelihood a wife will approve the purchase of expensive consumer electronics products”*

In the case of homelabs this specifically has a number of elements for consideration such as:

- Location
- Size
- [Noise](/housing/noise/)
- [Power](/housing/power/) & [Cooling](/housing/)
- Cost

The WAF can also be known (and arguably is known) more as the WTF, aka the Wife Tolerance Factor!

It is critical to [understand your requirements](/start-here/requirements/), in order to obtain maximum WAF.
