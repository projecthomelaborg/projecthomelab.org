[Nanolab](/start-here/nanolab/) is the nickname coined by Alex Galbraith for homelabs created using [Intel NUC](/hardware/intel-nuc/) and other small form factor devices, such as the Gigabyte Brix, and Apple Mac Mini.

It also refers to a [series on his blog](http://tekhead.it/blog/category/nanolab/) started in January 2013, which can be found [**here**](http://tekhead.it/blog/category/nanolab/).
