Nanolab is the nickname coined by Alex Galbraith for homelabs built from very small machines, starting with a [series on his blog](http://tekhead.it/blog/category/nanolab/) in January 2013 about running vSphere on the Intel NUC.

What was a novelty back then is now the most common way to build a lab. Intel has since left the NUC business and ASUS has taken over the line, but there are far more options today, including used business desktops and a wide range of new mini PCs. Three small nodes can make a quiet, capable cluster for less than the cost of one server from that era.

See [Mini PCs and 1-liter desktops](/hardware/mini-pcs/) for what to buy now, and the [archived Intel NUC article](/archive/intel-nuc/) for the history.
